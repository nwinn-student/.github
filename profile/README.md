Data Hobbyist

I enjoy looking for patterns in numbers and data, and focus on projects that either use or prepare for the use of such.
Performance tuning is also quite fun.

I daily lurk `luau-lang/luau` discussion/PR/issue sections, alongside the related `rfc`s and occassionally `lute`, as I use the Luau programming language in my projects.
I occassionally read through the Java `JEP`s.

I prefer to make software or formats tailored towards a particular usage case, so the below projects do not benefit everyone, but provide the intended users with an improved experience.

The projects I have worked on are listed below, alongside a brief on what I contributed to and a quick plug for the projects themselves:
- `Bench`: I worked on the conceptualization, low level design, and implementation.
  - I maintain this project as of 10/2026
  - A Luau benchmarking library that runs a provided function and inputs many times to acquire accurate performance measurements in time and space.
- `BufferSerializer`: I worked on the conceptualization, format design, low level design, and implementation.
  - I maintain this project as of 10/2026
  - A binary format tailored towards reduced output size through use of compression techniques whilst supporting backwards references.
- `InstanceStore`: I have worked on the conceptualization and design, of which neither are complete.
  - A `DataStore` abstraction that has yet to be completed.
